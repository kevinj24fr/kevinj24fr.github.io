01

One question, three tests

Intercellular communication is spatially organised, so it is incompletely represented once tissue is dissociated. The lab therefore works in living human cortex: organotypic slices preserve local relationships, electrophysiology and live imaging measure function, spatial and single-cell profiling resolve cell state, and quantitative models link the three without treating cells or spots as independent replicates.

An implanted electrode, a glioma, and amyloid are different insults. Each elicits a glial response that is communicated to neighbouring cells. The recurring question is whether that response merely accompanies injury or actively sets the tissue's next state. The research page lays out the platforms, the findings, and where this goes next.

02

Current threads

  1. Spatial organization of gliomas

    How IDH-mutant gliomas reorganize as they progress ({{L:Spatial analysis reveals the evolving|Cancer Cell 2026}}). Hypoxic niches that drive plasticity in low-grade glioma. Iron-metabolism stress programs that prime invasive mesenchymal glioblastoma states ({{L:Spatially restricted iron|Neuro-Oncology 2026}}).

  2. Tumor immune microenvironment

    Cell-type specific myeloid responses to hypoxia and acidosis in glioblastoma. Grade-independent immune subgroups and a T-cell recruitment deficit in meningioma ({{L:Immune Microenvironment Profiling|Brain and Spine 2026}}). Spatially resolved T-cell receptor profiling.

  3. Human brain tissue models

    Human brain slice cultures as a translational platform ({{L:Human brain slice cultures: translational|Life Science Alliance 2025}}). Injection-free, spatially controlled tumor delivery with soft cryogels ({{L:Injection-free|Biomaterials Advances 2026}}). Tumoroids checked against the tissue they came from.

  4. Neural implants and brain microinjury

    MicroFace: automated microglial morphometry that resolves spatial zones and bifurcated recovery trajectories after microinjury ({{L:MicroFace|iScience 2026}}). Biomarkers of the tissue response to chronic cortical electrodes. What flexible probes do to the transcriptome around them ({{L:Transcriptional characterization of the glial|Biomaterials 2021}}).

  5. Astrocytes and amyloid

    Whether astrocytic senescence drives amyloid pathology, following the finding that microglia carry amyloid-β into unaffected tissue ({{L:Microglia contribute|Nature Neuroscience 2022}}) and the map of human astrocyte development that diverges in glioblastoma ({{L:Mapping the developmental trajectory|Nature Cell Biology 2025}}).

  6. Diagnostics and neurosurgical translation

    CSF proteomics in spontaneous intracranial hypotension. FTIR and quantum-sensing routes to rapid neuro-oncology diagnostics (QuID-Neuro, DiaQnos). Riboflavin–UVA crosslinking of dura mater ({{L:Riboflavin|PLOS ONE 2026}}) and platelet-rich fibrin for dural closure.
