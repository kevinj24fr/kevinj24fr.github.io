Data

  • publications.json — papers and preprints with DOIs, authors, journal, year, and the lead-paper flags. Refreshed {{DATA_DATE}}.
  • openalex.json — per-DOI authorships with institutions and countries, and citation counts, from OpenAlex. Refreshed {{DATA_DATE}}.
  • trajectory.json — every Google Scholar entry with its kind and topic, the input to the trajectory strip. Refreshed {{DATA_DATE}}.
  • world.json — Equal Earth country outlines as SVG path data, from the R maps package.

How they are made is in the colophon.
