Mission

I lead an interdisciplinary research program where single-cell & spatial transcriptomics, AI, and neurotechnology converge. We aim to transform high-dimensional biological data into clinical insights and to advance fundamental understanding of how the brain responds to tumors, trauma, and intervention.

As a computational biologist, I build the tools to explore these data. As a neuroscientist, I collaborate to drive discovery across disciplines.

Research

Our lab, 3DBM&NE, works on glia and glioma systems biology: how brain tumors, trauma, and implanted devices reshape the cellular ecosystem of the human brain, and how to read that out with single-cell, spatial, and physiological measurements.

Spatial organization of gliomas

  • How IDH-mutant gliomas reorganize as they progress, mapped with spatial transcriptomics (Cancer Cell 2026)
  • Hypoxic niches that drive transcriptional plasticity in low-grade glioma
  • Iron-metabolism stress programs that prime invasive mesenchymal glioblastoma states (Neuro-Oncology 2026)
